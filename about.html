Who We Are

About Our Institution

Medical Career College of Health Sciences and Technology, Jos, located at No. 2 Rikkos, Jos, by TT & T Junction, Plateau State, is a forward-thinking institution committed to providing quality, practical, and professionally oriented education in the health sciences.

Situated in the serene and culturally diverse city of Jos, our institution is established with a clear vision: to contribute to the development of competent, compassionate, ethical, and innovative healthcare professionals capable of making meaningful contributions to healthcare delivery in Nigeria and beyond.

We recognize that the healthcare sector requires professionals who possess more than academic knowledge. Modern healthcare demands individuals with practical competence, critical-thinking abilities, strong communication skills, professional discipline, ethical consciousness, compassion, and a commitment to lifelong learning.

Our educational philosophy combines theory, practical skills development, laboratory-based learning, mentorship, professional development, and appropriate clinical exposure to prepare students for the realities of modern healthcare practice.

Our Roots

Our History

Foundation

Medical Career College of Health Sciences and Technology, Jos, was established with the objective of creating additional opportunities for individuals aspiring to pursue careers in the health sciences. The institution was founded on the understanding that Nigeria's growing population and changing healthcare needs require a continuous investment in the education and development of competent healthcare personnel.

Guiding Principles

From its foundation, the College has been guided by the principles of academic excellence, professional integrity, practical competence, discipline, innovation, and service to humanity.

Growth & Development

As we grow, our focus remains on strengthening academic structures, developing appropriate learning facilities, establishing meaningful academic relationships, promoting community engagement, and creating opportunities for students to pursue further education and professional development.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Philosophy

Our Vision

To become a recognized centre of excellence in health sciences education, producing competent, compassionate, innovative, and ethically responsible healthcare professionals who contribute meaningfully to the advancement of healthcare and society.

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical, and professionally oriented health sciences education through effective teaching, skills development, mentorship, innovation, research, community engagement, and adherence to ethical standards, thereby preparing students for meaningful careers and lifelong service to humanity.

Our Philosophy

We believe that quality healthcare education must connect knowledge with practice and professionalism with compassion. Healthcare professionals must be adequately prepared intellectually, technically, emotionally, and ethically to serve individuals and communities.

Our Approach

Teaching, Skills & Clinical Exposure

Teaching & Learning

Our approach goes beyond conventional classroom instruction. Students are exposed to classroom teaching, practical demonstrations, laboratory learning, skills development, clinical-oriented learning, professional mentorship, and community engagement. This integrated approach enables students to connect theoretical knowledge with practical applications.

Practical & Skills-Based Education

Healthcare is a practical profession. Our students are encouraged to develop competence through practical demonstrations, laboratory activities, simulation and skills-based training depending on their programme requirements. The objective is to ensure that students understand not only what they learn, but also how and when that knowledge can be applied appropriately.

Clinical & Professional Exposure

Where applicable and in accordance with programme and regulatory requirements, students are provided with opportunities for appropriate clinical and practical exposure through suitable healthcare facilities and professional environments. These experiences help students appreciate patient care, teamwork, communication, professional responsibility, documentation, ethics, and the realities of healthcare delivery.

Student Development

We believe that education is about developing the whole individual. Beyond academic instruction, students are encouraged to develop leadership skills, communication skills, teamwork, professional confidence, critical thinking, time management, ethical awareness, community responsibility, research interest, and entrepreneurial and problem-solving abilities.

Our goal is to graduate individuals who are prepared not only to enter the healthcare workforce but also to continue learning and developing throughout their professional lives.

Community Health & Social Responsibility

Medical Career College of Health Sciences and Technology, Jos, recognizes the important relationship between education and community development. Our institution is committed to supporting health promotion, disease prevention, health education, community awareness, and other appropriate healthcare-related initiatives.

Through community engagement and outreach activities, students can gain a deeper understanding of the social and environmental factors that influence health. We believe that a healthcare professional should understand the patient not only as an individual but also within the context of the family and community.

Staying Current

Research & Innovation

The healthcare sector is continuously changing. New diseases emerge, technologies advance, treatment approaches evolve, and healthcare systems face new challenges. We therefore encourage an institutional culture that values research, innovation, evidence-based practice, critical thinking, and continuous learning. We aim to encourage students and academic staff to develop an interest in research and to appreciate the role of evidence in improving healthcare outcomes.

Student Support & Mentorship

We understand that students require more than lectures to succeed. Our academic environment emphasizes mentorship, guidance, discipline, and constructive engagement between students and faculty.

Students are encouraged to seek academic guidance, participate actively in learning activities, and develop healthy professional relationships with lecturers and colleagues. We aim to create an environment where students feel supported while also being challenged to take responsibility for their academic and professional development.

Career & Professional Development

Our responsibility does not end with classroom teaching. We seek to prepare students for a professional environment in which they will be expected to demonstrate competence, responsibility, communication, teamwork, and ethical conduct.

Our long-term objective is to build graduates who can make meaningful contributions to hospitals, primary healthcare facilities, laboratories, public health institutions, research organizations, NGOs, government agencies, private healthcare organizations, and other relevant sectors.

Accreditation & Government Approval

Medical Career College of Health Sciences and Technology, Jos operates in full compliance with relevant regulatory and governmental bodies. Our programs are structured to meet national educational and healthcare standards, ensuring quality, credibility, and recognized certification.

These approvals reflect our commitment to maintaining the highest standards of healthcare education and institutional governance. We remain dedicated to transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement in all our academic operations.
